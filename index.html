Giving that fits into every paycheck

Most people want to give more. It just feels like too much, or it slips their mind. Chent makes the amount small and the habit automatic.

  1. Pick your charities

    Choose the causes you care about, from food banks to animal shelters. Split your gift between as many as you like.

  2. Set your amount

    Start at 1¢ of every dollar you earn, and raise it anytime. Chent sets aside your gift each payday.

  3. Watch it add up

    Chent combines small gifts into larger donations so less is lost to bank fees. You see every dollar delivered.
